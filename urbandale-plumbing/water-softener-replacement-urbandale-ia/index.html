    Metered Softener Replacement in Urbandale

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Urbandale, IA

    For homeowners in Urbandale, IA seeking an alternative to traditional salt-based systems, salt-free water conditioners offer a different approach. These units, sometimes called descalers, condition water to prevent mineral scale without removing the minerals or adding salt. While they do not produce soft feeling water, they reliably protect pipes and appliances from scale. We can replace your existing salt-free system or help you transition from a salt-based unit.
