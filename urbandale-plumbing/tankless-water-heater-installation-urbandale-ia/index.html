    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Urbandale, IA

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Water across much of Iowa comes from groundwater and runs very hard, so scale buildup in water heaters and fixtures is common. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.
