    PEX Pipe Replacement in Urbandale

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a common choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Hard freezes are a regular part of winter in Iowa, and pipes in exterior walls, garages, and crawlspaces are the most likely to burst. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during unexpected cold snaps. KFR Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers strong performance and water quality for your home.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Urbandale

    Gray polybutylene pipe was installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid-1990s. It breaks down from the inside when exposed to chlorinated water, and fittings can crack with no warning. Some insurers raise premiums or decline coverage on homes that still have it. We remove polybutylene supply lines and replace them with PEX-A or copper. A completed repipe can also help when selling the home.
