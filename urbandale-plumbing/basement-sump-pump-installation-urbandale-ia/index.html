    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Urbandale

    A great pump is useless if the water it moves runs right back toward the foundation. We route rigid PVC discharge pipe out of the basement and extend it at least 10 feet from the house or into a pop-up emitter or dry well. A spring or swing check valve keeps the column of water from dropping back into the pit after every cycle, which protects the motor. Frost goes deep into the ground across Iowa, so water and sewer lines must be buried well below the frost line. Where lines can freeze, we add a freeze-resistant outlet so ice cannot block the line.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Urbandale

    Pedestal sump pumps are an excellent choice for narrow or shallow sump pits, as their motor sits above the water level. This design makes them easier to maintain and can sometimes offer a longer lifespan because the motor stays out of the water. KFR Plumbing can expertly install pedestal sump pumps in your Urbandale home, with the right sizing and setup for effective water removal without crowding your pit.

    Sump Pump Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Urbandale, IA

    A high water alarm is an affordable part that gives you warning before water reaches the floor. We install standalone alarms or Wi-Fi monitors that send a text if the water level rises, the pump fails or power drops. These are popular with homeowners who travel or own second homes. We test the float trigger in front of you so you know how the alarm sounds.
