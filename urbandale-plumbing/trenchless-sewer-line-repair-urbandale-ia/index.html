    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Urbandale, IA

    Sewer lines in Urbandale, IA can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Clay soil shifts as it freezes, thaws, wets, and dries, which can crack older sewer lines and let roots inside. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Urbandale

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for extensive trenching. It is ideal for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Urbandale that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Urbandale

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.
