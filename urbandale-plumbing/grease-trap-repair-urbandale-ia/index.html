    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Urbandale, IA

    Tees direct flow into and out of the trap at the correct depth. Missing or broken tees allow floating grease to escape into the sewer line. Our service includes inspecting the condition of all baffles and repairing or replacing them as needed, often using durable, corrosion-resistant materials.
