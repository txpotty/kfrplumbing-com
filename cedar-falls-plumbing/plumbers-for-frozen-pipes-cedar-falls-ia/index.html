    Frozen Pipe Thawing in Cedar Falls

    When a faucet only trickles or nothing comes out after a cold night, ice is blocking the line somewhere. We open a faucet downstream to relieve pressure, then locate the frozen section with thermal imaging. Copper and galvanized pipes can be thawed with an electric pipe thawing machine that warms the metal from the inside. PEX and CPVC are warmed gently with heat guns or infrared heaters to avoid softening the plastic.

    Backflow Preventer and Irrigation Freeze Repair in Cedar Falls

    Backflow preventers and irrigation valves sit above ground and are exposed to the coldest air. Frozen backflow assemblies can crack their bodies or bonnets, causing leaks and failed tests. We repair or replace the assembly, insulate it with a fitted cover and suggest draining when deep cold is forecast. Many water providers require a passing backflow test after repairs, and we can set that up.
