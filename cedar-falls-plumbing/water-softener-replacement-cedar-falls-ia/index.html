    Brine Tank Replacement in Cedar Falls

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore consistent brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the best salt type for your system.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Alternatives in Cedar Falls, IA

    Some homeowners prefer a salt-free scale reduction system that uses template-assisted crystallization media. These systems do not remove hardness minerals but change them so they are less likely to stick. They require no drain and no salt. We explain the differences plainly, since they work differently than true softeners. They can be a good fit when drain access is limited or sodium is a concern.
