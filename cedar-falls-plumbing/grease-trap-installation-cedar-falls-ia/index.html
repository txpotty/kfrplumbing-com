    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in Cedar Falls

    The cost of grease trap installation in Cedar Falls, IA varies widely based on the type and size of the trap, whether it's an indoor or outdoor unit, and the complexity of the installation.     Determining the correct grease trap size involves calculating your kitchen's wastewater flow rate, fixture count, and peak operating hours.     The main purpose of a grease trap is to intercept fats, oils, and greases (FOG) from commercial kitchen wastewater before they enter the public sewer system. This prevents pipe blockages, protects municipal infrastructure, and helps businesses comply with environmental regulations.

    The cleaning frequency for a new grease trap depends on its size, the volume of FOG produced by your kitchen, and local regulations in Cedar Falls. In most cases, grease traps require cleaning every 1 to 3 months. Regular cleaning is essential to maintain efficiency and prevent backups.

    We calculate flow from every connected fixture, including sinks, floor sinks and dish machines. Hydromechanical units are usually rated by gallons per minute, while gravity interceptors are sized by volume and retention time.
