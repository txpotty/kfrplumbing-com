    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Des Moines

    Underground water line leaks are often hard to detect until significant damage has occurred.     PEX Water Line Repair in Des Moines, IA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option more and more used in homes across Des Moines, IA. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact. Our team is fully experienced in PEX repair, using specialized crimping tools and fittings to make strong, leak-proof connections. We ensure your PEX system continues to perform optimally.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Des Moines

    Whether your home has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our technicians are skilled in repairing both.     Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Des Moines, IA

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Water across much of Iowa comes from groundwater and runs hard to very hard, so scale buildup in water heaters and fixtures is common. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe rarely lasts because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Des Moines, IA

    Many older homes in Des Moines, IA still have galvanized steel water lines, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and reduced water flow over time. While replacement is often the best long-term solution, we can provide targeted repairs for individual leaks in galvanized pipes where practical. Our plumbers are comfortable working with these older systems, carefully assessing the pipe's condition and recommending the most effective repair strategy to extend its life or plan for eventual replacement.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Des Moines

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off fast in an emergency.

    A full break can flood a yard, wash out soil under walkways and leave your home without water.     Main Water Line Leak Repair in Des Moines

    Most main line leaks show up as a soggy strip of yard, a sudden jump in your water bill or a meter dial that moves with every fixture off. We confirm the leak by isolating the house shutoff and watching the meter. Then we use ground microphones to find the exact spot.
