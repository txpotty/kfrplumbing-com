    Dependable Water Softener Replacement Services in Des Moines, IA

    Brine Tank Replacement in Des Moines

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore consistent brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the best salt type for your system.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Des Moines

    Large households and homes with very hard water can benefit from a dual tank system that provides soft water all day and night. While one tank regenerates, the other stays in service. Kinetico and similar non-electric twin tank systems are popular for this reason. We size dual tank systems to your peak demand. They cost more upfront but avoid hard water bleed-through during regeneration.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Des Moines

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly exhausted. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Des Moines, IA

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and effective solution for hard water in Iowa. When replacing these units, we carefully disconnect the old brine tank and resin tank, then install the new components. We make sure all connections are leak-free and the drain line is properly routed. We will also provide tips on initial setup and salt refills for lasting soft water.

    Water Softener Failure Diagnosis in Des Moines, IA

    Before replacing, we check whether your softener has a simple fix like a clogged injector, failed motor or wrong settings. If the valve is obsolete or parts are no longer available, replacement is usually the better value. We test hardness before and after the softener to confirm the problem.     Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Des Moines

    A standard single tank softener replacement typically costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can add to the cost.

    Private wells serve many rural homes, and nitrates, iron, and bacteria make routine well water testing an important habit. Hard water contains dissolved minerals like calcium and magnesium. These minerals can lead to scale buildup in pipes, lower efficiency of water heaters and appliances, spots on dishes, and dry skin and hair. A working water softener is the best defense for combating these issues in Des Moines homes.

    Most residential water softener replacements can be finished within 3 to 6 hours, assuming no major plumbing changes are required. Our experienced plumbers work efficiently to minimize disruption, restoring soft water to your home on the same day.

    Salt-based softeners remove hardness minerals, calcium and magnesium, through ion exchange, effectively softening the water. Salt-free conditioners do not remove minerals but alter their crystal structure so they do not stick to surfaces. That prevents scale buildup without actually softening the water.

    While it might seem like a straightforward task, water softener replacement involves working with water lines, drain connections and sometimes electrical components.
