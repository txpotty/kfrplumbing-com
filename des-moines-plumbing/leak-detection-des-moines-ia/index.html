    Slab Leak Detection in Des Moines

    Slab leaks are a common concern for homes with concrete foundations, especially where soils shift. Our technicians use dedicated acoustic equipment and thermal cameras to pinpoint leaks under your home's foundation without destructive digging. Early detection of a slab leak prevents serious structural damage and mold issues, saving you significant repair costs down the line. We can locate leaks in both hot and cold water lines under the slab.

    Water Leak Detection in Walls & Ceilings in Des Moines, IA

    Water leaks behind walls or in ceilings can go unnoticed for long periods, leading to serious drywall damage, wood rot and mold. We use non-invasive methods like moisture meters, thermal cameras and borescopes to accurately pinpoint the source of these leaks. This approach minimizes the need for destructive investigation, saving you time and money on repairs to your home's walls and ceilings.

    Irrigation and Yard Leak Detection in Des Moines, IA

    Drip and sprinkler systems are a common source of hidden water loss in many yards. A cracked lateral or a stuck valve can waste thousands of gallons before anyone notices. We test the irrigation system separately from the house and trace leaks along buried lines. Many water providers recommend checking irrigation first when bills spike in summer.

    Main Water Line Leak Detection in Des Moines

    The service line between your meter and house can leak underground for months, especially in older galvanized or copper lines. Wet spots in the yard, oddly green grass or a sinking area near the front of the house are clues. We use line tracers to map the pipe and ground microphones to hear the leak. That keeps excavation to a small, targeted hole.
