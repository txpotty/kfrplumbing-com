    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Council Bluffs

    Iowa has many older homes and farmhouses with cast iron drains, galvanized supply lines, and clay sewer pipe. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are typical signs. Finding a leak early prevents structural damage and mold.

    Sewer Line and Drain Camera Inspection in Council Bluffs, IA

    A camera inspection shows the condition of your sewer line and main drains. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and buildup before they cause a blockage.     Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Council Bluffs, IA

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits significantly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to early tank failure.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Council Bluffs

    Water softeners fight hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance extends its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing expensive scale damage.

    Drainage Services and Hydrojet Plumbing in Council Bluffs

    Preventative drain maintenance is key to avoiding clogs and sewer backups.
