    Jet Pump Repair in Waterloo, IA

    Jet pumps, typically installed above ground, are often used for shallower wells or as booster pumps. Common issues include loss of prime, seal leaks, or motor wear. We troubleshoot problems like air in the lines, clogged foot valves, or faulty pressure switches. Our team in Waterloo is skilled at restoring prime and replacing worn components to get your jet pump operating efficiently again, a frequent need where water levels rise and fall.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Waterloo, IA

    Franklin Electric is a leading manufacturer of well pumps and motors, known for their durability. Our technicians are highly familiar with Franklin Electric products, from submersible motors to control boxes. We carry common Franklin Electric replacement parts to ensure efficient repairs. Whether it's a motor rewind or a complete pump replacement, you can trust our expertise with Franklin Electric systems in Waterloo.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Waterloo

    Submersible pumps are common in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms usually include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use specialized equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, protecting the integrity of your well system.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Waterloo

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Waterloo

    Grundfos pumps are popular for their energy efficiency and advanced technology. Our team is trained in the specifics of Grundfos well pump diagnosis and repair, including their variable speed drive systems. We address common problems like sensor failures, motor issues, or control system malfunctions. Count on KFR Plumbing for focused service that maintains the performance of your Grundfos well pump in Waterloo, IA.

    Well Pump Control Box & Electrical Repair in Waterloo, IA

    Many well pump failures stem from electrical issues within the control box or wiring. This can include tripped breakers, faulty capacitors, or corroded connections. Our licensed technicians are trained to safely diagnose and repair these complex electrical components. We ensure all wiring meets Iowa electrical codes, preventing hazards and ensuring reliable pump operation in Waterloo homes.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Waterloo, IA

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives steadier pressure at the tap.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Waterloo, IA

    Variable speed systems like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by adjusting motor speed. When they fault out, the drive typically shows a code that guides diagnosis. We troubleshoot drives, sensors and wiring and repair or replace components. These systems need a smaller pressure tank but careful setup.

    KFR Plumbing services and repairs all major brands of well pumps commonly found in Iowa, including Franklin Electric, Grundfos, Sta-Rite, Myers, Goulds, and Little Giant.     Well pump repair involves complex electrical components, high-pressure water systems, and often heavy equipment for pulling pumps from wells.     Common signs include no water, low water pressure, intermittent water flow, strange noises coming from the pump or pressure tank, cloudy or rusty water, or your pump running constantly. If you notice any of these symptoms in your Waterloo home, it's best to call a professional right away.

    The duration of a well pump repair depends on the issue. Simple fixes like a pressure switch replacement might take a few hours, while replacing a submersible pump can take a full day, especially if the well is deep. We strive for efficient service to restore your water as quickly as possible.
