    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Waterloo

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Waterloo

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Much of Iowa sits on rich, thick soil over glacial clay, which holds water and can push against basement walls and drain tile. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Waterloo

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab. This is a good option for newer homes or a first leak. If the home has had multiple leaks, we will explain why a full repipe may save money.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in Waterloo, IA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly popular choice for repiping projects in Waterloo, IA. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also highly resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in many water systems. We use quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a dependable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Waterloo

    Copper piping offers excellent durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Waterloo. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team carefully solders each joint, ensuring a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including changing temperatures and local water conditions. Copper also inherently inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.
