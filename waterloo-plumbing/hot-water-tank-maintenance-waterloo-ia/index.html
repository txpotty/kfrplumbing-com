    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Waterloo, IA

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial protector against corrosion. Water across much of Iowa comes from groundwater and runs hard to very hard, so scale buildup in water heaters and fixtures is common. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's badly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Waterloo, IA, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Costs in Waterloo, IA

    A yearly flush costs a small fraction of a new tank, and a new installed tank often runs $1,400 to $3,000 or more. We give you an straight read on whether maintenance is still worth it. If a tank is past 10 to 12 years old, shows rust at the base or leaks from the seams, we will tell you replacement is the smarter spend. When the tank still has life, maintenance keeps it running well for years.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Waterloo

    Hard water leaves calcium carbonate behind every time the burner heats the tank. Over a year that layer can harden into chunks that insulate the water from the flame and cause loud popping. We connect a hose to the drain valve, flush with full line pressure and agitate the bottom until the water runs clear. When scale is severe we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems. A clean tank heats faster and puts less stress on the bottom seam.

    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Waterloo, IA

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can block the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a stable blue flame and lower gas bills.

    Emergency Hot Water Tank Inspections in Waterloo
