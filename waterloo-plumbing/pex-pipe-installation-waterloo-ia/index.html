    Copper to PEX Conversions in Waterloo

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Private wells serve many rural homes, and nitrates, iron, and bacteria make routine well water testing an important habit. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more usually follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and lowers the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Waterloo, IA

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, similar to a breaker panel for water. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to weigh cost and performance.
