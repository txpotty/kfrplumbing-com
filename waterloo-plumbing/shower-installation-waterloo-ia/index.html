    KFR Plumbing stands behind our work with a comprehensive warranty on all shower installations, covering both labor and materials. The specific terms of the warranty will be outlined in your service agreement. We use durable products designed to last in local conditions.

    Plumbing rough-in usually takes 1 to 2 days, and trim installation takes a few hours. The full remodel timeline depends on tile and glass work.

    Pressure-balance valves keep the hot and cold ratio steady when pressure changes. Thermostatic valves control the actual temperature and allow separate volume control, which works well for multi-head showers.

    We install Moen, Delta, Kohler, Grohe, Hansgrohe and Brizo valves. We recommend pressure-balance or thermostatic valves for scald protection.

    A pressure-balancing valve maintains a consistent temperature by balancing incoming hot and cold water pressure, preventing sudden temperature changes. A thermostatic valve maintains a precise temperature setting regardless of pressure changes, offering a higher level of comfort and safety, especially in homes with children or elderly residents.

    Signs your old shower needs replacement include ongoing leaks that damage walls or floors, extensive mold or mildew that returns despite cleaning, severe cracks in the shower pan or enclosure, or outdated plumbing that constantly fails.
