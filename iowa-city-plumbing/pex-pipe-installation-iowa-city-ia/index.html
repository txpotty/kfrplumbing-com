    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Iowa City, IA

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is notorious for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Plenty of insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Iowa City, IA

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Iowa City, IA

    KFR Plumbing is knowledgeable in installing all types of PEX piping, including PEX-A, PEX-B and PEX-C, each with distinct properties for specific applications. We help you choose the best PEX type for your home's needs and budget in Iowa City, IA.     PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Iowa City

    PEX-B is stiffer and more affordable, and it is joined with copper crimp rings or stainless steel cinch clamps over brass or poly fittings. It is a solid option for smaller projects and budget-conscious repipes. Brands like Viega, Zurn and SharkBite offer dependable PEX-B systems. We use a go/no-go gauge on every crimp to confirm a proper seal.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Iowa City, IA
