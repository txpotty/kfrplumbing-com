    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Iowa City

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Iowa City for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit.     High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Iowa City

    If your current sump pump struggles to keep up with heavy water flow, especially during intense thunderstorms, it may be undersized. We offer high-capacity sump pump replacements and upgrades designed to handle significant water volumes. Our plumbers will assess your water intrusion patterns and recommend a pump with greater horsepower and a higher GPM (gallons per minute) rating.     Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Iowa City

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Iowa City, IA

    Sometimes it is not just the pump that needs replacing, since the sump basin or pit itself may be cracked, damaged or too small. KFR Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Iowa City, IA so your entire system works properly. A properly sized and intact pit is essential for the efficient operation and longevity of your new sump pump.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Iowa City, IA

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more long-lasting. We provide expert pedestal sump pump replacement in Iowa City, IA, with proper alignment and secure mounting.
