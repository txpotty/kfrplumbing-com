    Gas Burner and Venting Tune-Up in Iowa City, IA

    On gas tanks we clean the burner, inspect the thermocouple or flame sensor and look for soot, rust flakes or scorching around the combustion chamber. We check the draft hood and vent connector for proper slope and secure joints. Dust and lint from garages can block the flame arrestor screen on newer FVIR models, which causes the pilot to drop out. A clean burner gives a steady blue flame and lower gas bills.

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial defense against corrosion.     Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Iowa City

    Sediment Flushing & Cleaning in Iowa City, IA

    Over time, minerals and sediment settle at the bottom of hot water tanks, reducing efficiency and possibly leading to premature failure. Our maintenance includes a thorough flushing of the tank to remove these deposits. This process helps your tank heat water more evenly and quietly, a vital step for any hot water tank in Iowa City, IA, where mineral content in the water is a common concern.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Iowa City
