    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Iowa City

    PEX is flexible, resistant to scale and a popular choice for repiping older homes with failing copper or galvanized pipe. We install PEX with manifold or home-run layouts for balanced pressure. The material handles hard water well and can reduce noise in the walls. We patch access openings neatly or coordinate with your drywall contractor. A PEX repipe can stop recurring pinhole leaks.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Iowa City, IA

    PEX piping has become a popular choice for new plumbing installations and repipes in Iowa City, IA due to its flexibility, durability, and resistance to corrosion. KFR Plumbing expertly installs PEX piping for water supply lines, offering a cost-effective and long-lasting alternative to copper. Its ability to expand makes it more resistant to freeze damage than rigid pipe. Hard freezes are a regular part of winter in Iowa, and pipes in exterior walls, garages, and crawlspaces are the most at risk. We ensure all connections are secure and leak-free.
