    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Iowa City

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are commonly required for high-hazard applications in Iowa City. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. KFR Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with trusted brands like Watts and Febco.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Iowa City

    Most water providers require testable backflow assemblies to be certified at installation and then once a year. Our certified testers check each valve's opening point, relief valve operation and differential pressure. If a device fails, we can repair or rebuild it on the spot with genuine parts. We submit passing results so you avoid compliance letters or service interruption.
