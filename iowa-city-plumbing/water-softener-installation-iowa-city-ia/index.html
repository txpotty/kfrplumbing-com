    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Iowa City

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. KFR Plumbing expertly installs many models of salt-based softeners, making sure of proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Iowa City home.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Iowa City

    We also install Ecodyne water softeners, which are designed for robust performance and ease of use. These systems often come with user-friendly features and provide consistent soft water results. Our plumbers are familiar with Ecodyne specifications, guaranteeing a correct and efficient installation for your Iowa City home.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Iowa City

    Modern water softeners offer improved efficiency, using less salt and water during the regeneration process. We install a range of high-efficiency models that can lead to real savings on your utility bills. These systems are an smart long-term investment for any Iowa City homeowner concerned about resource consumption.
