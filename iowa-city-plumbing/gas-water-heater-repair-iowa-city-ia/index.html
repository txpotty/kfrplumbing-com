    Venting and Draft Repair in Iowa City, IA

    Atmospheric gas water heaters depend on a properly sloped metal flue to carry exhaust out of the home. Disconnected sections, rusted draft hoods and poor slope can let combustion gases spill back. We repair or replace vent sections and verify draft with a test. Proper venting is essential for your family's safety.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Iowa City

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it senses vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and shut down the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as needed.

    Power Vent Water Heater Repair in Iowa City

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust out through PVC pipe. Failures usually involve the blower motor, pressure switch, hot surface igniter or condensate in the vent. We read the error codes, test each part and repair the failure. Correct vent slope and termination are checked before we leave.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Iowa City, IA

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame signals a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats faster and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes falling from the flue.

    Tank Leaks & Pressure Problems in Iowa City

    Even gas water heaters can develop tank leaks, often due to corrosion from hard water or high water pressure. Private wells serve many rural homes, and nitrates, iron, and bacteria make regular well testing an important habit. While some leaks may call for a replacement, minor leaks or pressure relief valve issues can often be repaired. Our team assesses the extent of the damage, inspects the anode rod and addresses pressure imbalances to prevent further problems in your Iowa City home.

    Restoring Hot Water for Older Homes in Iowa City

    Many homes in Iowa City feature older gas water heater models that require specific knowledge for repair. Our technicians are familiar with the common issues and less common parts associated with these systems.
