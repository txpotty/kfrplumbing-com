    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Iowa City

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod set to your pit depth.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Iowa City, IA

    Power outages are common, especially during severe weather, and they leave your primary sump pump useless. We supply and install battery backup sump pump systems that switch on automatically if the power goes out, providing continuous protection for your basement in Iowa City, IA. Heavy spring rain and river flooding are common in Iowa, and saturated ground can quickly push water into basements. These systems include a deep cycle battery and a secondary pump, so your basement stays dry even when the electricity fails.

    Watch for a pump that runs constantly, cycles on and off rapidly, makes grinding noises or never seems to turn on. Rust stains, a stuck float and water sitting above the switch level are also warning signs.

    Manufacturer warranties typically range from 3 to 5 years on quality cast iron pumps. We also back our installation labor, and we will walk you through the specific coverage for the model you choose.

    We recommend having your sump pump inspected and maintained once a year, ideally before the rainy season. Regular maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning debris out of the pit, testing the check valve and making sure the discharge line is clear. This helps prevent unexpected failures and extends the life of your pump.

    Iowa uses the Uniform Plumbing Code as its statewide basis, with local jurisdictions handling many permits and inspections.     A standard basement sump pump installation typically takes 4 to 8 hours. If a new sump pit needs to be excavated, or there are unexpected challenges with plumbing or electrical access, the job might stretch to a full day. Our team works efficiently to limit disruption to your home.
