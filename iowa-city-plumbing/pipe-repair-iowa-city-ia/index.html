    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Iowa City

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in older homes and rusts from the inside until flow drops and leaks start at threaded joints. Repairs are possible, but every disturbed fitting can start a new leak. We replace failing sections with PEX or copper using dielectric unions to stop galvanic corrosion. For homes with widespread galvanized piping, we will quote a full repipe so you can compare.

    Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Iowa City

    Drain pipes made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. A leaking drain often shows up as a stain below a bathroom or a musty smell in a cabinet. We locate the leak, replace the damaged section and test the drain under full flow. We also check for proper slope and venting while the line is exposed.

    Leaky Pipe Repair in Iowa City, IA

    Even a small leak can lead to mold growth, wood rot and higher water bills over time. KFR Plumbing provides targeted leaky pipe repair services for all types of plumbing systems in Iowa City. We use modern leak detection equipment, including acoustic sensors and thermal imaging, to pinpoint the exact source of the leak without unnecessary demolition. Once located, we offer targeted repairs, from patching minor pinhole leaks to replacing corroded sections of pipe, for a watertight seal and long-term reliability.

    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Iowa City, IA

    PEX and CPVC are common in homes built over the last few decades. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic. CPVC becomes brittle with age and heat and can crack when bumped. We replace failed fittings and brittle sections with modern components rated for your plumbing.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Iowa City

    Older homes in Iowa City often have galvanized steel or aging copper pipes that are prone to corrosion, leading to discolored water, low water pressure and frequent leaks. KFR Plumbing provides full corroded pipe replacement services. We can replace problem sections or re-pipe your entire home with modern, durable materials like PEX or new copper, significantly improving water quality and system longevity. We keep disruption to a minimum during the replacement process.

    Pipe Leak Detection in Iowa City, IA

    Before any repair can happen, the leak needs to be found. KFR Plumbing offers modern pipe leak detection services in Iowa City, IA using non-invasive techniques. We use sophisticated equipment such as electronic listening devices, thermal cameras and inert gas tracers to precisely locate hidden leaks behind walls, under concrete slabs or underground. Our precise detection methods save you time and money by avoiding unnecessary demolition.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Iowa City

    Pinhole leaks form when copper pipe wall thins from the inside, often due to water chemistry, high velocity or poor grounding. They start as tiny drips that stain drywall or ceilings. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new pipe or a PEX transition. If we find several pinholes in the same area, we will talk with you about repiping that zone.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair in Iowa City, IA

    Slab leaks are especially challenging when the soil under a home shifts and stresses the pipes below it. Clay soil shifts as it freezes, thaws, wets, and dries, which can crack clay sewer laterals and let roots inside. Our plumbers are experts in diagnosing and repairing slab leaks beneath your home's concrete foundation. We use non-invasive methods like electronic leak detection to find the exact location, then offer solutions such as direct access repair, rerouting the line or trenchless techniques to fix the pipe with minimal impact on your property.
