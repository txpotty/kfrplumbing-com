    Root Intrusion Repair in Iowa City, IA

    Tree and shrub roots go right for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste. We cut the roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro-jetter, then repair or seal the joint so they cannot come right back.     Sewer Pipe Relining in Iowa City, IA

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is well suited for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Iowa City.
