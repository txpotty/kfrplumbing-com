    Soil type has a major effect on septic system performance. Basements are standard in most Iowa homes, so sump pumps, floor drains, and foundation seepage are common service concerns.     Frequent causes of septic tank failure include improper waste disposal, such as flushing non-biodegradable items, lack of regular pumping, tree root intrusion, heavy vehicle traffic over the drain field and poor soil drainage. Many rural Iowa homes rely on private wells, and KFR Plumbing can help with well pumps, pressure tanks, and related plumbing repairs.

    Most additives are not needed for a healthy system. Regular pumping, water conservation and keeping grease and wipes out of drains do much more.

Our technicians will provide an estimated timeline during the diagnosis.
