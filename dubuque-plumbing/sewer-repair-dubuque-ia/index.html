    Root Intrusion Repair in Dubuque, IA

    Tree and shrub roots go right for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste.     Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Dubuque

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Dubuque, IA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after years of wet weather. Clay soil shifts as it freezes, thaws, wets, and dries, which can crack clay sewer laterals and let roots inside. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.
