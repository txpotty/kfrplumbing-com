    French Drain Installation in Dubuque

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects water and carries it away from problem areas. It works well along foundations, behind retaining walls and in low spots that hold water after storms. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so fine soil does not clog the system. The drain is sloped to an outlet, dry well or permitted discharge point.

    Residential Drain Cleaning in Dubuque

    Slow drains or recurring clogs in your home are more than just an inconvenience; they can signal bigger issues. Our residential drain cleaning service in Dubuque uses tools like augers and hydro-jetting to clear blockages from kitchen sinks, bathroom drains, and laundry lines. We tackle common culprits like grease buildup, hair, and soap scum, restoring full flow and preventing backups, which are especially common in older homes with aging pipe systems.

    Dry Well Installation in Dubuque, IA

    Where there is no place to discharge water on the surface, a dry well lets it soak into deeper soil. Dense clay or hardpan layers can block absorption, so we test the soil and dig through hardpan when needed. Some jurisdictions have rules on dry well registration and placement, and we follow those rules. A properly sized dry well with a sediment chamber can handle repeated heavy storms.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Dubuque

    For the toughest clogs and stubborn buildup, our hydro-jetting service in Dubuque is the most effective solution. This method uses high-pressure water streams to scour the interior of your pipes, removing years of accumulated grease, scale, and debris. It's an excellent preventive maintenance tool, especially for commercial kitchens or homes with persistent scale problems, leaving your pipes clean and free-flowing.

    Channel and Trench Drains in Dubuque, IA

    Driveways and patios that slope toward the house can send storm runoff straight into a garage or under a door. A channel drain with a grate catches that sheet flow before it reaches the building. We use durable polymer or concrete channels from brands like NDS and set them flush with the surface. The channel ties into solid pipe that carries the water to a safe outlet.
