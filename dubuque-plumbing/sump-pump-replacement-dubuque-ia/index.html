    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Dubuque

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls.     Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Dubuque

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Dubuque, IA

    Zoeller pumps are renowned for their reliability and heavy-duty construction, making them a top choice for many Dubuque, IA homeowners. KFR Plumbing specializes in Zoeller sump pump replacement, so you get a high-quality, long-lasting unit.     Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Dubuque, IA

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more durable. We provide expert pedestal sump pump replacement in Dubuque, IA, with proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise is not a major concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.
