    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Dubuque

    Older properties may still have vitrified clay or even Orangeburg fiber pipe running to the street. Clay joints are open invitations for roots, and Orangeburg can flatten under soil weight. We clean these lines gently and use the camera to map joints and soft spots. If the pipe is failing, we will tell you plainly and show the footage. Cleaning keeps you running while you budget for a replacement.

    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Dubuque

    Trees and shrubs send roots toward any moisture they can find, and a sewer joint is a steady source during dry months. Thirsty yard trees and shrubs are common culprits in local yards. We use a sectional cable with a root saw sized to the pipe to cut the mass free and pull it out. Heavy root growth may need a follow-up with a jetter to clear fine root hairs. A camera inspection then shows where roots are entering so you can choose between lining or repair.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Dubuque, IA

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. Newer construction mostly uses PEX and plastic drains, while older homes often have a patchwork of materials from past repairs. Over time the inside of cast iron corrodes, scales and develops a channel along the bottom. Aggressive cutters can break thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting suited to older iron. This restores flow and gives a clear view on camera of the remaining wall. It also helps you decide if lining is a smart option.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Dubuque, IA

    Cleaning removes what is in the pipe, but it cannot fix a crack, a belly or a collapsed section. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera will usually show why. We compare the yearly cost of maintenance cleanings to a spot repair, lining or replacement. Sometimes regular cleaning is the sensible move for a few years. Other times a repair now saves more over time, and we show you the math.
