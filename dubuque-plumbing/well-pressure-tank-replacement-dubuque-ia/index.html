    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Dubuque

    When your well pressure tank shows signs of trouble, it is important to determine if a repair is practical or if full replacement is necessary. Small air leaks or issues with the air valve might be repairable. However, if the tank itself is corroded, has a ruptured diaphragm, or is waterlogged, replacement is almost always the more cost-effective and reliable option. Our technicians in Dubuque provide an straightforward assessment, explaining the pros and cons of repair versus a complete well pressure tank replacement, so you can make an informed decision.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Dubuque

    The tank tee is the manifold that connects the tank, switch, gauge, relief valve and drain. Galvanized tees corrode and restrict flow, especially with sandy or mineral-heavy water. We replace them with brass or stainless steel tank tees that hold up much longer. A new liquid-filled gauge gives reliable readings, and a fresh relief valve protects against a stuck switch. These small parts make a big difference in how well the system holds up.
