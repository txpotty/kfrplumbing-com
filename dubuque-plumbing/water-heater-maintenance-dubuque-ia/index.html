    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Dubuque

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Dubuque, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's heavily depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing costly tank leaks.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Dubuque, IA

    Gas water heater maintenance involves crucial safety inspections in Dubuque, IA. We check for gas leaks, proper venting of exhaust gases, and the function of the pilot light or igniter. We also clean the burner assembly and inspect the thermocouple. These checks are vital to prevent carbon monoxide exposure and keep your gas-fired appliance running safely.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Dubuque, IA

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building unsafe pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets replaced. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge if you have one.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in Dubuque

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for leaks.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Dubuque, IA

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as needed. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water quickly. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.

    Attic and Garage Water Heater Maintenance in Dubuque

    Many homes have water heaters in garages or attics, where heat, cold and tight access add stress to the unit. Attic units need working drain pans and drain lines to prevent ceiling damage if the tank leaks. We check the pan, drain line, venting and access platform. Garage units should be raised and protected where required for gas appliances. We confirm your setup meets local code.
