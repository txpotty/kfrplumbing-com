    Hydro-jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of pipes, thoroughly removing stubborn clogs, grease buildup and even tree roots.     Drain lines can break or collapse due to several factors, including shifting soil, tree root intrusion, corrosion from age, extreme temperature changes or heavy loads passing over underground pipes. Poor installation or defective materials can also lead to early failure.

    The length of drain repairs depends entirely on the issue. A simple clog can often be cleared in an hour or two. More involved repairs, like replacing a section of a main sewer line, might take a full day or even several days, especially if permits or extensive digging are involved.

    Minor clogs can sometimes be resolved with plungers or enzymatic drain cleaners. However, for stubborn blockages, recurring issues or suspected pipe damage, it is best to call a professional. DIY attempts with harsh chemical cleaners can sometimes damage pipes or mask a larger problem.

    The duration of drain repairs depends on the complexity of the problem. Simple clog removals might take an hour or two, while more extensive repairs like replacing a section of a main sewer line could take a full day or more. We'll give you an estimated timeline during our initial assessment.

    You should call a professional if your DIY efforts with a plunger or basic snake haven't cleared the clog, or if you have recurring clogs, foul odors or multiple slow drains. These issues usually point to a deeper problem that requires specialized tools and expertise to resolve effectively.
