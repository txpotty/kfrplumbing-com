    Tankless Water Heater Venting System Check in Ankeny

    Recirculation and Combi System Service in Ankeny, IA

    Many tankless units now include built-in recirculation pumps or buffer tanks to deliver faster hot water. These systems move more water through the exchanger and scale up sooner. We flush the recirculation loop, check pump operation and verify crossover or dedicated return line settings. Correct schedules can reduce wasted energy while still giving you quick hot water.

    Condensing Unit and Neutralizer Service in Ankeny

    High-efficiency condensing tankless heaters produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized before it goes down a drain. We inspect the condensate trap, line and neutralizer cartridge and replace the media when it is spent. A clogged trap can cause shutdowns or water leaking inside the cabinet. We also check that the line is protected where it runs outdoors.

    Commercial Tankless System Checks in Ankeny, IA

    Commercial tankless water heaters face higher demand and require more frequent, detailed maintenance. Our service for businesses in Ankeny includes detailed inspections of multiple units, checking for proper gas pressure, water flow rates and exhaust venting. We look for any signs of wear on components like igniters, sensors and heat exchangers, which is crucial for businesses that rely on consistent hot water, such as restaurants or salons.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Ankeny

    The heat exchanger in a tankless heater is a coil of small tubes that water races through while the burner fires. Hard water drops calcium and magnesium on those hot surfaces, narrowing the passages over time. We connect a submersible pump and hoses to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for 45 to 60 minutes. Then we flush with fresh water to rinse the system thoroughly. Restored flow means more stable temperatures and fewer shutdowns.

    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Ankeny

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading correctly and the burner breathing properly.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Tune-Ups in Ankeny, IA

    Noritz tankless water heaters are sturdy systems, but they still require annual tune-ups to maintain their performance through every season. Our maintenance includes checking the unit's exhaust system, confirming proper combustion air intake and verifying the integrity of the heat exchanger. We also calibrate the temperature and flow settings for the best efficiency and comfort, preventing common issues like cold water sandwiches.
