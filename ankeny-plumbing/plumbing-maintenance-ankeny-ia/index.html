    Comprehensive Plumbing Maintenance Services in Ankeny, IA

    Water Softener Maintenance in Ankeny

    Water softeners fight hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance extends its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing expensive scale damage.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Ankeny, IA

    Older sewer systems can back up during big storms, and backwater valves help keep sewage out of basement floor drains. A working sump pump is essential for homes with basements or crawl spaces when heavy rain overwhelms drainage. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve. That way your sump pump is ready to protect your home from flooding when sudden storms hit Ankeny.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Ankeny

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers call for annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Repiping and Polybutylene Replacement in Ankeny, IA

    Homes with polybutylene or aging galvanized pipe face a higher risk of leaks and low water pressure. During maintenance, we check pipe materials and look for signs of failure. If repiping makes sense, we explain PEX and copper options and give a written estimate. Repiping can be done in stages to fit your budget. New pipe brings back strong pressure and confidence.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Ankeny

    Regular water heater maintenance is key to preventing sediment buildup. Private wells serve many rural homes, and nitrates, iron, and bacteria make regular well testing an important habit. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, boosts efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.

    Drain Line Inspections for Maintenance in Ankeny

    As part of comprehensive plumbing maintenance, we offer drain line camera inspections. This lets us see the condition of your main sewer lines, identifying potential blockages from roots or grease buildup, or structural issues like cracks and offsets made worse by shifting soil. Clay soil shifts as it freezes, thaws, wets, and dries, which can crack clay sewer laterals and let roots inside. Early detection through inspection can prevent severe backups and costly emergency repairs.

    Residential Plumbing Service in Ankeny, IA

    Our complete residential plumbing maintenance covers every part of your home's system.     Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Ankeny

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get replaced. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility first.
