    Expansion Tank Sizing for Large Water Heaters in Ankeny

    A standard 2 gallon tank works for many 40 and 50 gallon heaters at moderate pressure. Larger heaters, higher incoming pressure or high-capacity systems may need a 4.5 gallon tank or larger. We use manufacturer sizing charts that factor in heater volume, supply pressure and temperature setting. Correct sizing keeps the tank from over-flexing and wearing out early.

    Tank Sizing and Placement in Ankeny

    Correct sizing and placement are key to an effective water heater expansion tank in Ankeny. An undersized tank will not provide adequate protection, while an oversized one is an avoidable expense. We carefully calculate the required tank volume based on your water heater's capacity, system pressure and average water temperature. Placement matters too, typically near the water heater on the cold water inlet line, with easy access for future maintenance or inspection.

    Expansion Tank for Tankless Water Heaters in Ankeny, IA

    While tankless water heaters do not store hot water, an expansion tank can still be beneficial, and sometimes required, in Ankeny, IA plumbing systems. Our plumbers assess your tankless setup and local codes to determine whether an expansion tank is necessary and recommend the ideal solution for your system's longevity and performance.
