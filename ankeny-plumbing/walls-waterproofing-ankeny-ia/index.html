    Newer construction mostly uses PEX and plastic drains, while older houses often have a patchwork of materials from past repairs.     Basement Wall Sealing in Ankeny, IA

    Basement wall sealing is a critical part of walls waterproofing. A long heating season keeps water heaters and boilers working hard, while humid summers can cause sweating pipes and damp basements. We apply high-quality sealants to porous concrete and masonry walls to prevent moisture from wicking through and causing dampness, mold, or musty odors. This service can be performed alone or as part of a larger waterproofing system, offering a drier and healthier environment in your Ankeny, IA basement.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Ankeny

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra attention before new tile is set.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Ankeny

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most effective method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Clay soil shifts as it freezes, thaws, wets, and dries, which can crack older sewer lines and let roots inside. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also integrated to divert water away.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Ankeny, IA

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already passed through the foundation walls. This typically involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Ankeny

    A slow drip from a shower valve, supply stub or drain fitting can soak a wall for months before you notice. We find the leak with moisture meters, thermal imaging and pressure tests. The damaged section of pipe or valve is replaced, and wet drywall is cut out and dried. Once the cavity is dry, we restore the wall and seal any wet-area surfaces.
