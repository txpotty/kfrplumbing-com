    Gas Water Heater and Tankless Line Upgrades in Cedar Rapids, IA

    Tankless water heaters often need up to 199,000 BTU, far more than the 40,000 BTU a typical tank heater uses. Many existing 1/2 inch lines cannot supply that demand, which causes error codes and lukewarm water. We calculate the full house load and upsize the line to 3/4 inch or larger as needed. This upgrade is one of the most critical steps in a successful tankless conversion.

    Flexible Gas Line (CSST) Installation in Cedar Rapids, IA

    The most common sign of a gas leak is the smell of rotten eggs, which comes from an additive called mercaptan. Other signs include a hissing sound near a gas line, dead vegetation over an underground gas line or an unusual increase in your gas bill. If you suspect a leak, get out immediately and call 911 and your gas company.

    CSST bonding connects the gas piping to the home's electrical grounding system to reduce damage from lightning strikes. It is a code and manufacturer requirement we follow on every job.

    We use trusted, industry-standard brands for gas line materials and fittings for durability and safety. This includes manufacturers like Gastite and OmegaFlex for CSST, and high-quality black iron pipe and fittings from reputable suppliers. All materials are selected to meet or exceed local plumbing codes.
