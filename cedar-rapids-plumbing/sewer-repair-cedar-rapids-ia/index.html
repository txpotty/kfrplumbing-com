    Pipe Burst & Collapse Repair in Cedar Rapids, IA

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Cedar Rapids

    Tree roots are a common culprit for sewer line damage in Iowa, attracted to the moisture and nutrients inside pipes. They can infiltrate tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog the line. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing root intrusion using camera technology. We can remove roots with specialized cutting tools and recommend preventive measures or durable pipe relining options to prevent future invasions, protecting your home's foundation.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Cedar Rapids

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can usually use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Cedar Rapids, IA

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Cedar Rapids, IA

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive option. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from extensive disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an efficient and popular choice for preserving property appearance in Cedar Rapids.
