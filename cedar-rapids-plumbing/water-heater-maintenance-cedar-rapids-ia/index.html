    Tank Water Heater Flushing in Cedar Rapids

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clean. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs quieter.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Cedar Rapids

    Tankless units are particularly sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures steady.
