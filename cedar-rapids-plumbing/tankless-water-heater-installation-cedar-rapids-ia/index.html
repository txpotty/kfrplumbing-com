    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Cedar Rapids, IA

    Signs you might need a new water heater, tankless or otherwise, include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, strange noises from the unit or frequent leaks. For tankless systems specifically, frequent error codes or a significant drop in hot water flow could point to a need for service or replacement.

    In Iowa, plumbing and often gas or electrical permits are typically required for tankless water heater installation. KFR Plumbing handles all required permit applications and makes sure your installation passes all required inspections by local authorities.

    Size depends on how many fixtures run at once and your incoming water temperature. Many homes need a unit rated around 7 to 10 gallons per minute.

    With annual descaling, tankless units often last 15 to 20 years. Skipping maintenance in hard water areas can shorten that considerably.

    Yes, gas tankless water heaters require specific venting materials and configurations different from traditional tank heaters. Many modern units use PVC or polypropylene for venting, and proper slope and termination are critical for safe and efficient operation. Electric units do not need venting.
