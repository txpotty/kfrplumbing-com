    Yard and Area Drain Cleaning in Cedar Rapids

    Yard drains and area inlets collect runoff from lawns, rock landscaping and patios. Over time the grates fill with leaves and the pipes fill with silt and small gravel. We pull the grates, vacuum the inlet box and jet the pipe toward its outlet. This restores the line so it can handle a full storm. We also check that grates are secure and level.

    Storm Drain Camera Inspection in Cedar Rapids

    Before and after cleaning, or to diagnose the cause of persistent blockages, KFR Plumbing offers storm drain camera inspection in Cedar Rapids. Using waterproof cameras, we can see the interior of your storm drain lines to identify the exact nature and location of blockages, assess pipe condition, and check for structural damage like cracks or collapses. This non-invasive diagnostic tool gives precise problem identification and lets us provide the most effective and targeted cleaning or repair solutions.

    Root Removal from Storm Drains in Cedar Rapids

    Tree roots are a common cause of severe storm drain blockages in Cedar Rapids, especially in older neighborhoods with mature trees. Roots seek out moisture and can infiltrate drain pipes through tiny cracks or loose joints, growing and eventually completely blocking water flow. KFR Plumbing uses dedicated root-cutting attachments on our drain snakes and hydro jetting equipment to cut through and remove root intrusions. After removal, we can recommend further steps to prevent future root problems.

    Hydro Jetting for Storm Drains in Cedar Rapids, IA

    Hydro jetting is a proven solution for deeply clogged storm drains in Cedar Rapids, IA. This method uses high-pressure water streams to wash out stubborn obstructions like compacted soil, heavy silt, roots, and grease buildup. It is very effective for thoroughly cleaning the inside of the pipe walls, restoring the drain to its full capacity and preventing a quick return of blockages, a common issue with older drainage systems.
