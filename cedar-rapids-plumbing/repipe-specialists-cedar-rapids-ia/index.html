    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Cedar Rapids

    Copper piping offers excellent durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Cedar Rapids. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed.     Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Cedar Rapids

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures.     PEX Pipe Replacement in Cedar Rapids, IA

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. KFR Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Cedar Rapids, IA, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points. PEX also significantly dampens water hammer noise, providing a quieter plumbing system for your home.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Cedar Rapids

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Basements are standard in most Iowa homes, so sump pumps, floor drains, and wall seepage are common service concerns. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.
