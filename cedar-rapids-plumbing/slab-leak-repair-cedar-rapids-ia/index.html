    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Cedar Rapids

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is severely deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an effective long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely.     Slab Leak Reroute in Cedar Rapids
