    Control Valve and Injector Service in Bettendorf, IA

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a full regeneration every time.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Regeneration Optimization in Bettendorf, IA

    The resin beads are responsible for ion exchange, removing hard minerals from your water. Over time, these beads can become fouled with iron or other contaminants, reducing their effectiveness. Our maintenance includes cleaning the resin bed and adjusting the regeneration cycle settings for maximum softening capacity. We make sure your system is regenerating efficiently for your water quality, providing truly soft water.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Maintenance in Bettendorf

    The cost of water softener maintenance in Bettendorf, IA generally ranges from $100 to $250 for a standard service. This can vary based on the system's age, brand and any specific repairs needed. For a firm quote, please contact KFR Plumbing to assess your unit.

    A brine tank full of water usually means a clogged injector, a stuck float or a drain line problem. Our service clears the cause and resets the system.

    Maintenance usually includes inspecting the brine tank for salt bridges, cleaning the tank, checking and cleaning the resin bed, lubricating moving parts, sanitizing the system and running diagnostic checks on the control valve. We also test water hardness to confirm effectiveness.

    We service Culligan, Kinetico, EcoWater, Pentair, Fleck and Clack based systems, Whirlpool, GE and most other brands. We can source most valve parts fast.

    While you can perform basic tasks like checking salt levels and refilling the brine tank, professional maintenance involves inspecting internal components, testing resin efficiency and diagnosing control valve issues.
