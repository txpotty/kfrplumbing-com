    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Bettendorf, IA

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more long-lasting.     Zoeller Sump Pump Replacement in Bettendorf, IA

    Zoeller pumps are known for their reliability and heavy-duty construction, making them a top choice for many Bettendorf, IA homeowners.     Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Bettendorf

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Bettendorf for their quiet operation and ability to sit fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit and install a new, appropriately sized pump. We make sure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and maximize efficiency. That is crucial for handling heavy rainfall.

    Sump Pump Basin and Pit Replacement in Bettendorf, IA

    Sometimes it is not just the pump that needs replacing, since the sump basin or pit itself might be cracked, damaged or too small. KFR Plumbing offers sump pump basin and pit replacement in Bettendorf, IA so your entire system works properly.     Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Bettendorf

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves without binding. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Bettendorf, IA

    For added protection, many homeowners in Bettendorf choose a combination sump pump system, which pairs a primary electric pump with a battery backup pump in one unit. This keeps the system running even if the power fails or the primary pump quits. KFR Plumbing can replace your existing combination system or install a new one, evaluating your needs for groundwater management. We make sure the components are well matched for strong performance and longevity.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Bettendorf

    If your current sump pump struggles to keep up with heavy water flow, especially during intense thunderstorms, it may be undersized. We offer high-capacity sump pump replacements and upgrades designed to handle significant water volumes. Our plumbers will assess your water intrusion patterns and recommend a pump with greater horsepower and a higher GPM (gallons per minute) rating.     Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Bettendorf

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins.
