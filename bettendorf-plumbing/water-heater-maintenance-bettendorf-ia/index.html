    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Bettendorf, IA

    Tankless water heaters need specific maintenance, mainly a descaling flush to remove mineral deposits that can restrict flow and reduce efficiency. Our technicians in Bettendorf, IA use commercial-grade solutions to clean the heat exchanger, so your unit keeps providing on-demand hot water efficiently.     Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Bettendorf

    Tankless units are especially sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures steady.

    Tank Water Heater Flushing in Bettendorf

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium sediment at the bottom of the tank. This layer insulates the water from the burner or lower element, wasting energy and causing popping sounds. We connect a hose to the drain valve and flush the tank until the water runs clear. For heavy buildup, we may stir and flush in several cycles. A clean tank heats faster and runs more quietly.

    Electric Water Heater Element Service in Bettendorf, IA

    Electric water heaters use upper and lower heating elements controlled by thermostats. Sediment can coat the lower element and cause it to fail. We test both elements and thermostats with the power off and replace parts as needed. A failed element is often the reason for lukewarm water or running out of hot water fast. Proper testing avoids replacing parts that are still good.
