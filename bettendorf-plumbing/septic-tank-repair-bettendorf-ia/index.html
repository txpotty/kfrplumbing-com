    Septic Baffle and Tee Repair in Bettendorf

    Baffles and tees keep scum and solids in the tank so they do not flow out into the leach field. Concrete baffles crumble from hydrogen sulfide gas, and plastic tees can break off. When they fail, solids clog the field quickly. We replace damaged baffles with durable PVC tees and add an effluent filter where appropriate.

    Leach Field Repair in Bettendorf, IA

    Soggy ground, strong odors or lush grass over the field can point to a struggling disposal area. Causes include clogged distribution pipes, a crushed distribution box or soil sealed by solids. We jet clean lines, replace distribution boxes and determine whether the field can be restored. If replacement is needed, we explain the design and permit steps.

    Drain Field Clog Remediation in Bettendorf

    A clogged drain field is a common cause of septic system backups and can be a serious issue for homeowners in Bettendorf. This can be caused by excessive solids, tree root intrusion or soil compaction. We offer professional drain field remediation, which may include hydro-jetting the distribution lines, addressing root infiltration or assessing the soil's percolation capacity to restore proper effluent absorption and prevent future flooding.
