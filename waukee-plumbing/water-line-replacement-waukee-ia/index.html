    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Waukee, IA

    Polybutylene pipe was used in some homes built in the 1980s and 1990s. It can become brittle and fail without warning. Many insurers list it as a risk. We replace polybutylene service lines with durable materials. This lowers the chance of sudden leaks.

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in homes built before the 1970s. Over time, it corrodes inside and chokes the pipe. This causes low pressure and rusty water. We replace galvanized lines with modern PEX or copper. The difference in water pressure is often immediate.
