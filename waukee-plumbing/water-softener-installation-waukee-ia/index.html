    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Waukee, IA

    Municipal water often contains chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter paired with a softener removes those chemicals and protects resin. We install combination systems with filter and softener tanks or dual-stage units. This setup improves both water quality and feel. Many homeowners notice a real difference in taste.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Waukee

    For homeowners looking for an alternative to traditional softeners, we also offer salt-free water conditioner installation. These systems condition the water to prevent scale buildup without removing healthy minerals or adding salt. While not true softeners, they are an effective option for scale prevention and are popular in many Waukee households.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Waukee

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. KFR Plumbing carefully installs many models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Waukee home.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Waukee, IA

    Softener installation costs depend on system type, capacity and whether you need a loop. Installing on an existing loop is faster and costs less than adding a new loop. Salt-free systems and combination filters vary in price. We give you a written quote that includes equipment, labor and setup. We also explain long-term salt and maintenance costs.

    Softener Loop Installation in Waukee

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not. We cut into the main line, install the loop and add a bypass valve. We keep outdoor hose bibs and irrigation on hard water when possible to save salt. A proper loop makes future service simple.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Waukee

    Reverse osmosis systems remove dissolved solids, which can be high in some water supplies. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet. Some systems also connect to refrigerators or ice makers. RO pairs well with a softener for complete water treatment. We change filters on a schedule so the system keeps working well.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Waukee

    Accurate testing is key for proper softener settings. We test your water for hardness, iron and chlorine before installation. Then we program regeneration frequency and salt dosage. This helps reduce salt use while keeping water soft. We review settings with you so you understand how the system works.
