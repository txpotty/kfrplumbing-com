    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Waukee

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method skips the problem area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Waukee with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Waukee

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is simplest. Reroutes eliminate future leaks in that section of pipe.
