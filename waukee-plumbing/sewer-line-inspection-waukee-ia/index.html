    Cast Iron and Clay Pipe Condition Assessment in Waukee

    Many older homes have cast iron drain lines under the floor and clay sewer laterals outside. Cast iron can corrode and develop scale, cracks and channeling along the bottom of the pipe. Clay joints shift and let roots in. Our camera inspection shows how much life the pipe has left so you can plan repairs on your timeline.

    Belly and Offset Detection in Waukee, IA

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds water and waste. Offsets happen when pipe sections shift out of alignment, often because of soil movement. Much of Iowa sits on rich, deep soil over glacial clay, which holds water and can push against basement walls and drain tile. The camera shows these defects clearly, and we mark their location for targeted repair.

    Detecting Pipe Damage and Corrosion in Waukee, IA
