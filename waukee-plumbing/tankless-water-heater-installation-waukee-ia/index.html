    Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Waukee, IA

    Electric tankless water heaters are an good option for homes in Waukee where natural gas is not available or preferred.     Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Waukee, IA

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Water softeners are a standard upgrade in Iowa homes, helping protect tank water heaters, faucets, and appliances from mineral damage. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides extra protection.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Waukee

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Waukee, IA

    Tankless installation costs more upfront than a standard tank because of the unit price, gas line upgrades and venting changes. Over time, lower standby energy losses and a longer expected lifespan offset part of that cost. Tankless units often last 15 to 20 years with proper descaling, compared with about 8 to 12 years for many tanks in hard water areas. We give you a side-by-side quote so you can decide what suits your budget.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Waukee

    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units. A 40 gallon tank heater uses roughly 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need up to 199,000 BTU, so the gas line usually needs to be upsized. Venting changes from a metal flue to PVC or stainless, and the unit moves to a wall location. We remove and haul away your old tank and patch the old flue penetration.
