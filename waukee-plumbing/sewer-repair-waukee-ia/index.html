    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Waukee

    Tree roots are a common culprit for sewer line damage in Iowa, drawn to the moisture and nutrients inside pipes. They can infiltrate tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog the line.     Sewer Line Backups and Blockages in Waukee

    Sewer backups are often caused by severe blockages from grease buildup, foreign objects or heavy tree root intrusion. Our team uses advanced sewer camera inspections to identify the exact cause and location of the blockage.     Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Waukee

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's much more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and stopping recurring clogs. This service is especially helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Waukee dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with recurring drain issues.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Waukee, IA

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is well suited for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Waukee.
