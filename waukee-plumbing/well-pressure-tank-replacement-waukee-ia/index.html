    Pressure Switch Replacement in Waukee, IA

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop, typically at 30/50 or 40/60 psi. Pitted contacts, insect nests and a clogged sensing nipple are common causes of failure in well houses. We replace worn switches with Square D or similar models and clean or replace the nipple below it. A low-pressure cutoff switch can also be added to stop the pump if the well runs dry.     Bladder Pressure Tank Replacement in Waukee

    Bladder tanks hold water inside a flexible butyl bag surrounded by pressurized air. When the bag ruptures, water fills the whole tank, there is no air cushion and the pump short cycles. A quick test is pressing the air valve on top, since water spitting out means the bladder has failed. We replace the tank, set the new pre-charge and confirm the pump runs a full cycle. Most bladder tank swaps take about two to three hours.

    Constant Pressure System Conversion in Waukee, IA

    A constant pressure system pairs a variable speed drive like the Franklin SubDrive or Grundfos SQE with a smaller pressure tank. The pump speeds up or slows down to hold a set pressure, so showers do not fade when a sprinkler kicks on. These systems cost more, often $2,500 to $5,000, but they end pressure swings. They also cut down on hard starts on the motor. We explain whether your well and pump are good candidates.
