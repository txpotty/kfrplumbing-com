    Yes, weather can affect sewer lines. Around Sioux City, sharp temperature swings in spring and fall can loosen fittings and expose weak spots in older plumbing.     Roots typically enter through existing gaps at joints, then expand as they grow. Over time they can crack clay pipe, separate joints and create a full blockage. Cutting roots helps short-term, but sealing or replacing the joint is the lasting fix.
