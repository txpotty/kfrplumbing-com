    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Sioux City

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. KFR Plumbing carefully installs various models of salt-based softeners, making sure of proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Sioux City home.

    Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Sioux City, IA

    Municipal water often contains chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter paired with a softener removes those chemicals and protects resin. We install combination systems with filter and softener tanks or dual-stage units. This setup improves both water quality and feel. Many homeowners notice a big difference in taste.

    Water Softener Replacement in Sioux City, IA

    Older softeners can lose efficiency due to worn resin, failing valves or clogged injectors. We remove the old unit and install a new, more efficient model. Newer metered valves regenerate only when needed, saving salt and water. We check the loop and drain line during replacement. A new softener can restore full performance.

    Softener Loop Installation in Sioux City

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not. We cut into the main line, install the loop and add a bypass valve. We keep outdoor hose bibs and irrigation on hard water when possible to save salt. A proper loop makes future service easy.
