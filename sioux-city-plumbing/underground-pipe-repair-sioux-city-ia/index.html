    Pipe Bursting Services in Sioux City, IA

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless technique where a new pipe is pulled through the path of the old, broken pipe, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is highly effective for complete pipe replacement, creating a new, durable pipe of the same or larger diameter. It's a solid solution for severely damaged or collapsed sewer and water lines, offering a long-term fix in Sioux City, IA.

    Underground Water Line Repair in Sioux City, IA

    A leaking underground water line can lead to high water bills, reduced water pressure and even foundation damage, a serious concern for any home.     Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Sioux City

    Galvanized steel pipe rusts from the inside, and polybutylene pipe becomes brittle and splits without warning. If your underground service is either material, repairs are often temporary. We replace the line with PEX or Type K copper, often by pulling the new pipe through the old path to limit digging. A new line restores pressure and removes the risk of repeated leaks.

    Main Water Line Repair in Sioux City

    Your main water line runs from the meter box to the house and is under constant pressure. When it leaks, you may notice a spinning meter dial with all fixtures off, low pressure or a wet spot that never dries. We locate the leak, expose the pipe and splice in a new section with approved fittings. If the line is old galvanized or polybutylene, we will recommend a full replacement before more breaks happen.
