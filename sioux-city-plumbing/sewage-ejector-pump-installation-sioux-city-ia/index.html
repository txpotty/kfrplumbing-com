    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Sioux City

    While both sump pumps and sewage ejector pumps remove water, their applications are distinct. KFR Plumbing provides clear guidance and installation for both in Sioux City. A sump pump handles groundwater and storm runoff, typically at a basement's lowest point to prevent flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is designed for wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in below-grade plumbing. We make sure you get the correct pump for your drainage needs, preventing costly mistakes.

    Signs that you might need a new sewage ejector pump include unusual noises from the pump or pit, frequent cycling on and off, slow drainage from basement fixtures or a sewage odor coming from the area.
