    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, especially in closed-loop systems. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to ensure proper system operation and prevent premature wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Ames.     Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Ames

    The cost of water heater installation in Ames, IA typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of water heater (tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed plumbing or electrical modifications. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your specific needs.

    Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit and an age of 10 to 15 years or more. If you notice any of these issues, it is best to have a professional inspection.

    Rusty water, leaks around the base, rumbling noises and running out of hot water quickly are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement is often the smart move.

    To improve efficiency, ensure your new water heater is properly sized for your home, consider a tankless model for on-demand heating, insulate hot water pipes and set the thermostat to 120°F (49°C) to prevent scalding and reduce energy consumption.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. KFR Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, ensuring peace of mind. We will discuss exact warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the ideal size.

    Most standard water heater installations, particularly for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours. However, a conversion from a tank to a tankless system, or installations requiring significant plumbing or electrical modifications, might take a full day or longer. We will provide an estimated timeline during our initial assessment.
