    Frequently Asked Questions About Bathroom Plumbing in Ames

    The cost of bathroom plumbing repair in Ames can vary widely depending on the issue. A simple faucet repair might be $150 to $300, while a more involved toilet replacement could range from $400 to $800, including the new fixture. We provide a detailed estimate after diagnosing the problem.

    A failing pressure-balance cartridge often causes temperature swings when someone flushes a toilet or runs a faucet. Replacing the cartridge usually fixes it.

    If the toilet is cracked, uses more than 1.6 gallons per flush or needs frequent repairs, replacement is often better. A new WaterSense toilet can reduce your water bill.

    Common signs include dripping faucets, running toilets, slow or clogged drains, reduced water pressure in showers, strange noises from pipes, and visible leaks around fixtures or under sinks. Any of these symptoms call for a professional inspection to prevent further damage.

    While some minor issues like replacing a showerhead might be DIY-friendly, most bathroom plumbing problems are best handled by a professional. Incorrect repairs can lead to worse leaks, water damage, or even code violations. For anything beyond the simplest fix, calling KFR Plumbing is safer and often more cost-effective in the long run.

    Most repairs take one to two hours. Toilet replacement takes about two hours, and shower valve replacement usually takes half a day.

    Most drips come from a worn cartridge or seal. Hard water scale can damage cartridges and cause leaks sooner. Replacing the cartridge typically solves the problem.

    Minor repairs like replacing a faucet or toilet usually do not require a permit.
