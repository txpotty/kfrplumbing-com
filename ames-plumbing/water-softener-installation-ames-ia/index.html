    Installing on an existing loop usually takes two to three hours. Adding a new loop can take half a day or more.

    Maintenance mainly involves periodically adding salt to salt-based systems, usually every 4 to 8 weeks depending on water usage and hardness.     Common signs of hard water include soap scum buildup on fixtures and shower doors, dry skin and hair, dull laundry and scale buildup inside pipes and appliances like water heaters. We can perform a water hardness test in your Ames home to verify your need for a softener.
