    Soil type has a big effect on septic system performance. Much of Iowa sits on rich, deep soil over glacial clay, which holds water and can push against basement walls and drain tile.     Frequent causes of septic tank failure include infrequent pumping, which lets solids accumulate, tree root intrusion into pipes or the tank, excessive water usage overloading the system, driving heavy vehicles over the drain field and using harsh chemicals that kill beneficial bacteria.
