    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Ames

    Homes from the late seventies through the mid nineties may contain gray polybutylene supply pipe, which is prone to failure. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents structural damage and mold.

    Drain Line Inspections for Maintenance in Ames

    As part of comprehensive plumbing maintenance, we offer drain line camera inspections. This lets us see the condition of your main sewer lines, identifying potential blockages from roots or grease buildup, or structural issues like cracks and offsets made worse by shifting soil. Clay soil shifts as it freezes, thaws, wets, and dries, which can crack clay sewer laterals and let roots inside. Early detection through inspection can prevent severe backups and expensive emergency repairs.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Ames

    Tankless water heaters need specialized maintenance, mainly descaling to remove mineral buildup from the heat exchanger. This is especially important where the water is hard. Our technicians use approved descaling solutions to keep your tankless unit running at peak efficiency, preventing reduced hot water flow and possible breakdowns. We also check gas lines, ventilation, and water connections.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Ames

    Regular water heater maintenance is crucial to preventing sediment buildup. Water softeners are a common upgrade in Iowa homes, helping protect tank water heaters, faucets, and appliances from mineral damage. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements. This extends the life of your unit, boosts efficiency, and helps avoid sudden cold showers or leaks.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Ames

    Water softeners fight hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance extends its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing expensive scale damage.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Ames

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get replaced. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility first.

    Drainage Services and Hydrojet Plumbing in Ames

    Preventive drain maintenance is key to avoiding clogs and sewer backups. We offer routine drain camera inspections to identify grease buildup, roots, or other obstructions before they cause a problem. Our hydrojet plumbing service can blast away stubborn blockages, keeping your drainage system clear and free-flowing, which is especially important for homes with mature trees near the sewer line.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Ames, IA

    Heavy spring rain and river flooding are common in Iowa, and saturated ground can quickly push water into basements. A working sump pump is vital for homes with basements or crawl spaces when heavy rain overwhelms drainage. Our maintenance includes testing the float switch, checking for proper discharge, cleaning the pit of debris, and inspecting the check valve. That way your sump pump is ready to protect your home from flooding when unexpected storms hit Ames.
