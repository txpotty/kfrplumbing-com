    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Davenport, IA

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to premature tank failure.

    Drain Line Inspections for Maintenance in Davenport

    As part of complete plumbing maintenance, we offer drain line camera inspections. This lets us see the condition of your main sewer lines, spotting potential blockages from roots or grease buildup, or structural issues like cracks and offsets made worse by shifting soil. Clay soil shifts as it freezes, thaws, wets, and dries, which can crack clay sewer laterals and let roots inside. Early detection through inspection can prevent severe backups and costly emergency repairs.

    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Davenport

    Newer construction mostly uses PEX and plastic drains, while older houses often have a patchwork of materials from past repairs. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents structural damage and mold.

    Water Heater Maintenance in Davenport

    Regular water heater maintenance is key to preventing sediment buildup. Private wells serve many rural homes, and nitrates, iron, and bacteria make regular well testing an important habit. Our service includes flushing the tank, inspecting the anode rod for corrosion, checking the temperature and pressure relief valve, and examining all connections and heating elements.     Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Davenport

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work.
