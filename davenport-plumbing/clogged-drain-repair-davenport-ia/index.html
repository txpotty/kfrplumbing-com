    Most common household clogs, like those in sinks or showers, can be cleared within one to two hours. More severe blockages, such as main sewer line clogs requiring camera inspection and hydro-jetting, might take a few hours to a full day, depending on the complexity.

    Water softeners are a common upgrade in Iowa homes, helping protect tank water heaters, faucets, and appliances from mineral damage. Hard water leaves mineral scale that narrows pipe walls and gives grease and hair something to grab. Older homes may also have cast iron pipes that rust and roughen inside.     You should call a professional plumber for clogged drain repair if DIY methods like plungers are ineffective, if you experience multiple clogged drains, or if you suspect a main sewer line blockage. Recurring clogs or slow drains also indicate a need for professional diagnosis to prevent bigger issues.

    Common signs of a clogged drain include slow drainage, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets, unpleasant odors coming from your pipes, and water backing up into sinks, tubs, or showers. If multiple drains are affected, it often points to a main line issue.

    Most standard clogged drain repairs, such as those in a sink or bathtub, can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. More complex issues, like main sewer line clogs or those requiring camera inspection and hydro-jetting, might take 3 to 4 hours or longer, depending on the severity.
