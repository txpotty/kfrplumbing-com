    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Marion, IA

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside.     Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. A long heating season keeps water heaters and boilers working hard, while summer humidity can cause sweating pipes and damp basements. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth the price.
