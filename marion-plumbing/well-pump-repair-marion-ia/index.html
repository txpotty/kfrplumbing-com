    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Marion, IA

    Many well pump issues come from electrical problems, including tripped circuit breakers, faulty wiring, or a malfunctioning control box. These can prevent the pump from starting or cause it to run erratically. Our licensed plumbers are also skilled in electrical diagnostics, safely testing circuits, relays, and capacitors to identify and repair electrical faults.     Pressure Tank Repair in Marion, IA

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives more even pressure at the tap.

    Jet Pump Repair in Marion, IA

    Jet pumps, typically installed above ground, are often used for shallower wells or as booster pumps. Typical issues include loss of prime, seal leaks, or motor wear. We troubleshoot problems like air in the lines, clogged foot valves, or faulty pressure switches. Our team in Marion is skilled at restoring prime and replacing worn components to get your jet pump operating efficiently again, a frequent need where water levels rise and fall.
