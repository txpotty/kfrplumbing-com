    Pool Water Leak Detection in Marion, IA

    Pools lose a lot of water to evaporation in summer heat, so a leak can hide behind normal loss. We use a bucket test to compare evaporation with actual water loss. Then we pressure test pool plumbing and dye test fittings, lights and skimmers. Finding the leak safeguards the pool and the soil around it.

    Hidden Wall & Ceiling Leak Detection in Marion, IA

    Leaks behind walls or in ceilings can cause significant damage before they become visible, leading to stained drywall, peeling paint or a musty odor. These often stem from faulty pipe connections, old galvanized pipes or issues with shower pans and drains. Our technicians use moisture meters and infrared cameras to scan these areas, identifying elevated moisture levels and temperature differences that indicate a hidden leak, minimizing the need for extensive wall demolition.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Marion

    Leaks in underground water lines, from your meter to your home, can go unnoticed for extended periods, wasting water and causing yard erosion. Signs include oddly green patches in your lawn, persistent wet spots or a constantly running water meter. KFR Plumbing uses ground-penetrating radar and tracer gas detection methods to accurately map out and pinpoint leaks in buried pipes, allowing for targeted excavation and repair rather than digging up your entire yard.

    Water Heater and Softener Leak Detection in Marion

    Water heaters and softeners can leak at fittings, valves and drain lines. A softener stuck in regeneration can send water down the drain nonstop, and a dripping relief valve on a water heater wastes water too. We check these units carefully and repair or replace failed parts. That often explains a mystery bill in homes with water softeners.

    Smart Leak Detection Device Installation in Marion

    Smart shutoff valves like Moen Flo and Phyn monitor water flow and can shut off water automatically when they detect a leak.
